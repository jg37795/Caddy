Pro Mode breakdown
How it works
Single-tap the map to set your aim target. Set Front / Middle / Back, then tap the green to mark its edges. The chip under the big number always says what your target is — Middle of green, Pin with its offset from the middle, or Layup target, where the number is the yards that point leaves to the middle along your shot line. Restore Green re-applies an imported course's edges; Clear Green removes them. Tap the wind pill (top right) for full wind detail and what it does to this shot. During a round, drag the flag pin to fine-tune where your shot started before tapping Finish. Plays-like folds in elevation, wind, temp, altitude & humidity.
